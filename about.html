Meet John O'Reilly

John has been in the car business for as long as he can remember, and he still gets excited by the sound of a well-tuned engine. He started Wheels on Fire to share his collection with people who care about cars as much as he does. He admits he's a perfectionist, more with cars than with people, if he's honest.

Why Wheels on Fire?

  • Every car is inspected by John himself, usually twice
  • Honest advice to help you find the perfect car
  • A collection polished like a freshly waxed car
